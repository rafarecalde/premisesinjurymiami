Restaurant, Bar, and Nightclub Injuries

Part of the Miami-Dade premises injury library from Recalde Law Firm, P.A.

A restaurant or bar injury is usually a premises claim about the floor, the furniture, the crowd, or the security plan. It is not automatically a claim for serving alcohol.

Section 768.125, Florida Statutes, generally does not make a bar liable for serving an adult. The statute has two narrow exceptions, involving willful and unlawful sale to a person under 21 and knowingly serving a person habitually addicted to alcohol. A spilled drink on a dance floor is analyzed under section 768.0755. An assault in a room the club packed past what the exits could handle is a premises and security question. The apartment presumption in section 768.0706 does not apply to a nightclub.

South Beach, Wynwood, and Brickell venues overwrite cameras quickly. A receipt, a wristband, and a short video from a phone are often what is left by the next afternoon.
