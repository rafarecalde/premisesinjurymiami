Hotel, Resort, and Pool Injuries

Part of the Miami-Dade premises injury library from Recalde Law Firm, P.A.

A Miami hotel injury often involves more than one company. The entity on the sign may be a brand. The building may be owned by an investment group. A manager may employ the housekeeping staff. A contractor may maintain the pool or the elevators. Each relationship is in a contract, and each contract points at an insurance policy.

Guests are invited onto the property. The operator owes them reasonable care to keep the premises reasonably safe and to warn of hidden dangers. Pool decks, lobby tile after rain, balcony guards corroded by salt air, and valet lanes are recurring settings in Miami Beach, Brickell, and Sunny Isles. Spills inside a hotel restaurant can fall under section 768.0755. A broken deck drain or a loose railing is a condition of the property, not a transitory spill.

Out-of-town guests should not assume the deadline matches the law of their home state. For most Florida negligence claims that arose after March 24, 2023, section 95.11 allows two years. Photograph the condition before the hotel corrects it.
