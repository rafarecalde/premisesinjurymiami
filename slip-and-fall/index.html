Slip and Fall Injuries in Miami

Part of the Miami-Dade premises injury library from Recalde Law Firm, P.A.

Call 305-792-9100 Free Consultation

A slip and fall claim in Florida turns on the condition that caused the fall and on who controlled that part of the property. A puddle in a grocery aisle is analyzed differently from a broken sidewalk or a missing handrail.

For a transitory foreign substance in a business — a spill, tracked-in water, or a leak that just appeared — section 768.0755, Florida Statutes, requires proof that the business had actual or constructive knowledge of the condition. A permanent defect, such as a cracked tile, a torn mat that stays down for months, or a raised sidewalk slab, is a different kind of hazard. The knowledge rule for spills does not replace the ordinary duty to maintain the property.

Miami-Dade adds a practical problem. Rain blows into lobbies. Air conditioning drips onto tile. Cruise-day crowds track water through restaurants in Brickell and on Miami Beach. Photograph the floor before it is mopped, and keep the shoes you wore. If the property is a city, the county, or another government defendant, section 768.28 adds a written notice step before suit.
