Premises Liability in Miami-Dade

The rules that come up on the first call. The Florida law library goes deeper on each one.

Florida law requires the person who controls property to use reasonable care to keep it reasonably safe for invited visitors, and to warn of hidden dangers they know about or should discover by reasonable inspection. When a hazardous condition causes an injury, more than one person or company may share responsibility.

Who May Be Responsible

  • Property owners of the land and the building
  • Property managers hired to operate and maintain it
  • Commercial tenants who control the space where you were hurt
  • Condominium and homeowners associations responsible for common areas
  • Maintenance contractors for cleaning, landscaping, elevators, and repairs
  • Contractors whose work creates a hazard for people passing by

Rules That Change the Case

  • Section 768.0755 requires proof that a business knew or should have known about a spill or other transitory substance.
  • Section 768.81 bars recovery when the injured person is more than 50 percent at fault.
  • Section 768.0706 is a security presumption for certain multifamily buildings, not for stores or nightclubs.
  • Section 768.28 sets notice rules, a waiting period, and damages caps for cities, the county, and other government property.
  • Section 95.11 gives most negligence claims that arose after March 24, 2023, two years to file.

Evidence moves faster than the deadline. For most private-property negligence claims after March 24, 2023, the filing deadline is two years under section 95.11. Surveillance video is often overwritten in days. Photograph the condition, get the incident report, and keep the shoes you were wearing.

What a Claim Can Include

Depending on the facts, Florida premises claims can include medical expenses, lost wages, loss of earning capacity, and pain and suffering. Fatal injuries are addressed under the Wrongful Death Act, sections 768.16 through 768.26. Every case turns on the injury, the proof, comparative fault, and the insurance that applies. How damages work and how coverage is layered are covered in the library.
