Parking Garages, Sidewalks, and Pickup Zones

Part of the Miami-Dade premises injury library from Recalde Law Firm, P.A.

Call 305-792-9100 Free Consultation

Many serious Miami falls happen between the front door and the car: garage ramps, valet stands, hotel porte-cocheres, and sidewalks lifted by roots. The legal question is who maintained that strip of ground.

A private garage may be owned by a tower, operated by a vendor, and cleaned by a third contractor. A valet stand may be staffed by a company that does not own the curb. A rideshare pickup zone is usually the property owner's design problem when the claim is about lighting, a broken curb, or a lane with no place for a person to stand. A driver who strikes a pedestrian is a separate auto claim. The app company is not automatically the premises owner.

Public sidewalks, county garages, and airport curbs can belong to a government entity. Those claims follow section 768.28, including written notice before suit. Private sidewalks the adjacent owner controls are ordinary premises claims, with the two-year period in section 95.11 for negligence that arose after March 24, 2023.
