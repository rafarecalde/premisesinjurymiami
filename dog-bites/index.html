Dog Bites on Miami Property

Part of the Miami-Dade premises injury library from Recalde Law Firm, P.A.

Call 305-792-9100 Free Consultation

Section 767.04, Florida Statutes, makes a dog owner liable for bites to people lawfully on the property, with a limited defense when a clearly readable "Bad Dog" sign is posted. The statute is about the dog owner. A separate premises question can exist for a landlord, an association, or a short-term host when the property's rules, gates, or common areas are part of how the bite happened.

Miami rentals and condominiums see both: a dog in a hallway the association controls, and a dog inside a unit or a vacation-rental yard. Local breed bans are no longer the framework Florida uses. The facts that matter are where the bite occurred, whether the injured person was lawfully there, who owned the dog, and what the property had already been told.
