Florida Premises Law

Part of the Miami-Dade premises injury library from Recalde Law Firm, P.A.

Call 305-792-9100 Free Consultation

These guides stick to the Florida statutes and bar rules that decide premises claims. They are general information, not a prediction about any one injury.

The provisions readers use most often are section 768.0755 on transitory substances in businesses, section 768.81 on comparative fault, section 95.11 on the two-year negligence deadline for claims that arose after March 24, 2023, section 768.28 on government property, and section 768.0706 on multifamily security. Medical-bill evidence is addressed in section 768.0427.

Fee division between law firms, if it happens after a consultation with Recalde Law Firm, P.A., requires the client's written consent under Rule 4-1.5 of the Rules Regulating The Florida Bar. The firm does not litigate injury cases.
